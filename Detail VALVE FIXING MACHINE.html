VALVE FIXING MACHINE

It is FAB-O-MECH’s high-performance solution for the automated valve assembly process in rubber inner tube production. Engineered for high-speed, repeatable precision, this machine integrates advanced sensor technology to ensure accurate valve core positioning and secure locking for various tube types, including motorcycle, bicycle, and automotive applications.

Designed specifically for post-curing operations to streamline workflow, the FOM-VF-Series minimizes setup time and eliminates the inconsistencies of manual assembly. It is a trusted asset for rubber component manufacturers looking to maintain strict quality control and maximize production efficiency.
