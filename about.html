Company Overview

Engineered in Thane.
Trusted Across India.

FAB-O-MECH ENGINEERS was established in 2004 with a clear mission: to design and manufacture industrial tube manufacturing machinery that competes on a global scale while thriving on the rigorous Indian factory floor. Operating from our strategic manufacturing base in the Jai Mata Di Compound, Kalher.

We have spent over 20 years perfecting the engineering behind automobile inner tube production. Today, our machines are the backbone of production lines for leading manufacturers throughout India and beyond.
