H-250 HYDRAULIC SPLICER

The H-250 Hydraulic Splicer is a specialized industrial solution designed by FAB-O-MECH ENGINEERS for the high-speed splicing of inner tube ends. Engineered for demanding rubber processing environments, the machine utilizes a sophisticated PLC-controlled system with a 4.3-inch display to manage precise butting and clamping forces. It is powered by a 5 HP, 1440 RPM hydraulic power unit, providing a maximum butting force of 2651 kgf and a clamping force of 785 kgf.

Designed to maximize production throughput, the H-250 features a sinking-type knife carriage mounted on linear motion guides with three-speed adjustment, ensuring clean, uniform cuts for seamless joining. The system supports a wide range of flat tube widths from 75mm to 250mm and includes a 240-liter reservoir with a heat exchanger to maintain optimal operational temperatures. This machine is an essential asset for manufacturers requiring repeatable, high-quality tube bonds and increased hourly output capacity.
